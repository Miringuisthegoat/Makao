Got questions?

Frequently Asked

How does the one-time fee work?
You pay once per listing and it stays live on Makao until you mark it as rented or delete it. There are no monthly charges, no renewal fees, and no commission on the rent you receive.
When does my listing go live?
Your listing goes live immediately after payment is confirmed. For M-Pesa this happens as soon as you enter your PIN and the Safaricom callback confirms the transaction. For card and PayPal payments, Stripe confirms instantly via webhook.
Can I edit my listing after publishing?
Yes. You can edit your listing's title, description, photos, price, and availability at any time from your host dashboard — no extra charge.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept M-Pesa (STK push via Safaricom Daraja), Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and PayPal — all processed in Kenya Shillings (KES). Card and PayPal payments are handled securely by Stripe.
What's the difference between Landlord and Agency?
The Landlord package is for individual property owners listing a single property at a time. The Agency package is designed for real estate agencies — it covers up to 10 listings per payment, includes a Verified Agency badge, top search visibility, homepage featuring, and a full analytics dashboard.
Can I buy boosters for a Landlord listing?
Yes. Boosters are available to all hosts regardless of package. You can purchase them individually from your dashboard at any time after your listing is live.
